    Questions

    The honest answers.

    Do I need to know how to code?

    No. You describe, Unwebbed builds. If you can write a text message you can direct a build — "make the hero warmer", "add their opening hours" — and the site updates in about a minute.

    Where do the leads come from?

    Public map data from Overture Maps, with OpenStreetMap as a fallback. Unwebbed looks for signs of an existing website and shows whether that check is complete, so you can review each lead before you call.

    What do the sites cost to build?

    Your budget is real AI cost, passed through — a full site with images is typically 40–80 cents. What you charge the client is yours; most freelancers charge a few hundred dollars per site.

    How do the sites go live?

    One click publishes the site on Unwebbed hosting at its own secure address, so you can show it live before anyone pays. When the client says yes, you send them one bill — your price plus 1, 2 or 3 years of hosting — and the moment they pay, their domain is registered in their name and the site goes live on it. You can also download any site as a file and host it anywhere.

    The domain is theirs: we hold it on their behalf and hand it over on request. Hosting never renews on its own — they get reminders at 90, 30 and 7 days, then two weeks of grace before the site shows a notice.

    What do I earn on the hosting?

    Your site price in full, plus $90 on a 1-year package, $150 on 2 years, or $195 on 3 years. Your earnings are recorded the moment the client pays, and Finley pays them out to you; automatic Stripe payouts are not available yet. Renewals earn the same share again, for as long as you stay their designer.

    What do I get for free?

    Searching is free forever — every town, unlimited, on every plan. Signing up adds trial build credit so you can make your first sites without a card. Pick a plan when you want to keep building.

    Which plan should I start on?

    Solo. It does the whole job — find the businesses, build the sites, write the pitches, work the call sheet — and you hand the finished file to your client. Move up when waiting starts to annoy you: Studio builds four sites at once, publishes them live in a click, and preps fifteen leads in one run. Upgrading takes one click and nothing changes about how you work.

    Can the client change the site later?

    Yes — you stay their designer. Open the project, tell the builder what to change ("new hours", "add the spring menu"), republish, and the link they already have updates. Edits typically cost 10–15¢.

    Is my list private?

    Yes. Your leads, notes, and sites live in your account and nobody else can see them. No ads, no selling data — the privacy policy is one page and in plain English.

    Not ready to start? Keep me posted.

    One short email when something worth knowing happens — new towns, new features, real results from real designers. No newsletter, no schedule.
