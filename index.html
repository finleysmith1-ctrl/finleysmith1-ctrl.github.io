The honest answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe, Unwebbed builds. If you can write a text message you can direct a build⁠—“make the hero warmer”, “add their opening hours”⁠—and the site updates in about a minute.

Where do the leads come from?

Public map data (OpenStreetMap), checked against the live web. A lead only makes your list after Unwebbed has looked for their website and found nothing worth clicking.

What do the sites cost to build?

Your budget is real AI cost, passed through⁠—a full site with images is typically 40–80 cents. What you charge the client is yours; most freelancers charge a few hundred dollars per site.

How do the sites go live?

One click publishes to your own free Netlify account, or download the site as a folder and host it anywhere. The link belongs to you and your client⁠—not to us.

Worth knowing before several clients are live: Netlify’s free plan is capped at 300 credits a month and a publish costs 15 of them, so roughly twenty publishes⁠—shared across every site on that account, along with visitor traffic. Go over and Netlify pauses all of them until the month resets. Fine for your first few clients; after that, give each client their own free account (in their name, which you want anyway) or use a host without that cap.

What do I get for free?

Searching is free forever⁠—every town, unlimited, on every plan. Signing up adds trial build credit so you can make your first sites without a card. Pick a plan when you want to keep building.

Which plan should I start on?

Solo. It does the whole job⁠—find the businesses, build the sites, write the pitches, work the call sheet⁠—and you hand the finished file to your client. Move up when waiting starts to annoy you: Studio builds four sites at once and publishes them live in a click, Studio does four at a time and preps fifteen leads in one run. Upgrading takes one click and nothing changes about how you work.

Can the client change the site later?

Yes⁠—you stay their designer. Open the project, tell the builder what to change (“new hours”, “add the spring menu”), republish, and the link they already have updates. Edits typically cost 10–15¢.

Is my list private?

Yes. Your leads, notes, and sites live in your account and nobody else can see them. No ads, no selling data⁠—the privacy policy is one page and in plain English.
