Slow on purpose.

The dough takes as long as it takes. A long, cold, natural ferment isn't a bottleneck we haven't fixed — it's the entire point. It's what turns flour, water and salt into something with a crackling crust, an open crumb, and a flavor you can't rush into existence with more yeast and less time.

We could bake faster. We'd rather bake right, sell out, and start again tomorrow.
