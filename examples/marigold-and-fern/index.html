Nothing on the shelf stays put.

Every arrangement starts at the flower market, not a lookbook. When ranunculus give way to garden roses, or the first cool week pushes dahlias out for chrysanthemum and smoke bush, the counter changes with them — whether or not anyone asked it to.

That means the bouquet you loved in March won't exist in July. It also means what you get in July is better: fuller, stranger, actually grown here and now.
