If you're nervous, start here.

You can tell us you're anxious. That's the whole first step.

We slow down. We explain what's about to happen before it happens, and we check in as we go. If you need a minute, you get a minute — there's no reward for gritting your teeth through it.

Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is a surprise. That's the extent of the promise, and we mean to keep it every visit.

Kids are welcome, really

No clenched-teeth waiting room, no bracing for a fight. We keep things simple and short for younger patients, and we talk to kids the same way we talk to their parents — plainly, and without tricks.

If it's a first visit, tell us. We'll build the appointment around that.
